These are the supplied poster files, copied here under readable names. The
originals (photo_2026-09-07_11-49-*.jpg) are still in the project root.

To add another upcoming film: drop its poster here, then add an entry to the
UPCOMING array in js/data.js with `poster:` pointing at it and `trailer:`
pointing at a web-encoded copy in assets/trailers/.

Portrait artwork works best - the card crops to roughly 0.72 (288x400), and all
four supplied posters are already close to that ratio.
